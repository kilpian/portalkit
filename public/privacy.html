Your Privacy at PortalKit

Effective date: May 11, 2026

The short version

We collect your name, email, and usage data to run PortalKit. Your payment info goes directly to Stripe — we never see your card number. We don't sell your data. Ever. Here's the full picture.

What we collect

  • Name and email — provided when you create an account. Used to identify you and send you important account emails.
  • Payment method — handled entirely by Stripe. PortalKit never stores or sees your card number. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and actions taken inside the app. Helps us understand what's working and what to improve.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We never share your data with advertisers.
  • We never use your clients' information for anything other than delivering your portal.

Third-party services we use

PortalKit relies on the following services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy governing data they collect directly.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal data at any time. Email us at hello@getportalkit.com and we'll respond within 5 business days.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. After you cancel, we retain it for 30 days before deleting it — giving you time to change your mind or export anything you need.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email hello@getportalkit.com. We're happy to help.
