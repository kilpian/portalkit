Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 11, 2026 · Kilpian LLC, doing business as PortalKit

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use PortalKit, we collect the following types of information:

  • Name and email address — collected at account registration and used to identify your account and communicate with you.
  • Payment information — billing is handled entirely by Stripe. We never store, see, or have access to your credit card details. Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data.
  • Usage data — we collect information about how you interact with the PortalKit platform, including pages visited, features used, and actions taken, to improve the product.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the PortalKit service.
  • To send transactional emails such as account confirmations, invoices, and important service updates.
  • To improve and develop the product based on how users interact with it.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you about your account.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share your data only with the following trusted service providers, and only to the extent necessary to provide the service:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant and handles all billing data directly.
  • Brevo — transactional email delivery (account notifications, receipts).

We may also disclose your information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies only. These cookies are strictly necessary for the operation of PortalKit — specifically for session management so you remain logged in while using the platform.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies without your explicit consent.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, we will retain your data for 30 days after the cancellation date, after which it will be deleted from our systems.

You may request earlier deletion at any time — see Section 6 for details.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, email us at hello@getportalkit.com and we will respond within 5 business days.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you may have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. Children

PortalKit is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with their information, please contact us at hello@getportalkit.com and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. For any material changes — meaning changes that significantly affect your rights or how we use your data — we will notify you by email prior to the change taking effect.

Minor updates may be posted to this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:
